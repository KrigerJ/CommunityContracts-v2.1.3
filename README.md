Community Contracts v2.1.3

Turn Stardew Valley into a thriving cooperative economy by partnering with townspeople through contracts, automation, and on‑demand services. Version 2.1.3 is the largest expansion since the mod’s creation — introducing powerful combat utilities, centralized processing, new automation systems, improved UI navigation, and expanded service options. This update builds on the streamlined, dialogue‑free workflow of v2.1.2 while adding entirely new layers of gameplay depth.

Getting Started

    Install SMAPI if you haven’t already.
    Download and install Community Contracts.
    Launch Stardew Valley and press V to open the Community Contracts Toolbar.
    You can change the hotkey anytime from the Settings Menu on the toolbar.
    All features, services, and configuration options are accessible directly from the toolbar.
    See the Getting Started video HERE.

What’s New in v2.1.3

Shockwave Bombs (Combat + Mining Utility)
Unleash a customizable shockwave that damages monsters and breaks rocks in the mines.

    Toggle Shockwave mode directly from the toolbar
    Shockwave originates from the point where you left‑click with your weapon
    Powered by items stored in your Fuel Locker
    Each blast consumes one fuel item
    Shockwave radius and damage scale with:
        NPC friendship levels
        The value of fuel items Build friendships and use higher‑value fuel to create devastating shockwaves.

Warp Service (Custom Warp Library)
Create and manage your own warp‑to locations.

    Save custom warp points anywhere in the world
    Add warp points to Skull Cavern levels to preserve progress
    All warp locations are stored in your config and persist across saves and new farms Build a personal fast‑travel network tailored to your play style.

Centralized Processing Service

All raw‑goods processing is now unified into a single service.

    Recycling
    Juicing
    Pickling
    Sashimi
    Ore processing 
    Run everything at once with one contract. 
    Choose delivery to your Delivery Chest or directly to the Shipping Bin.

Processing Chest (High‑Capacity Input Storage)

A dedicated chest for all raw goods used in processing services.

    Frees up backpack space
    Simplifies automation management
    Supports:
        Recycling
        Juicing
        Pickling
        Sashimi
        Ore processing
        Seed making
        Crab pot baiting 
        The Processing Chest becomes your centralized hub for all raw materials.

Improved Character Menu (Scrolling Support)

The Character Menu now supports scrolling via:

    Mouse wheel
    Keyboard up/down arrows 
    This makes navigating large NPC lists smoother and more intuitive.

Enhanced Delivery Options

Collecting services now allow you to choose whether NPCs deliver:

    Raw goods
    Processed goods To either:
    Your Delivery Chest
    Your Shipping Bin

Animal Care Service

Hire a character to:

    Feed your animals
    Pet your animals A fully automated livestock‑care workflow.

Recycling Service
Hire a character to recycle trash items for you — fully integrated with the Processing Chest. 

Rock Candy Manufacturing 
Rock Candy becomes a powerful new automation option.

    Hire a character to manufacture Rock Candy using items in your Processing Chest
    Total item value must meet or exceed the value of one Rock Candy
    Any leftover value is given to the NPC as a tip Rock Candy remains a universal favorite gift and a powerful consumable.

Combat Shielding
Enable Shielding in Community Contracts Settings to gain daily monster‑damage protection.

    Pay a small daily fee
    Shielding HP scales with total friendship points

Return Damage
Enable Return Damage to reflect a portion of incoming monster damage back to nearby enemies.

    Pay a small daily fee
    Damage reflection scales with total friendship points
